We are building a marketplace platform for booking celebrities, influencers, athletes, musicians, creators, and other public figures for personal events and commercial purposes.

Users can discover people by category, search/filter by location, price, audience, language, availability, and service type, then open a detailed profile with photos, bio, social accounts, ratings, services, pricing, and a real-time calendar synchronized with the celebrity's availability.

Services can include Instagram/TikTok promotions, posts, stories, personalized birthday/wedding videos, event appearances, weddings, performances, brand campaigns, commercials, and other custom requests.

The booking flow is: discover a celebrity → select a service → check available time slots → submit requirements → book/pay or send a request → celebrity/manager accepts, rejects, or proposes changes → booking is confirmed and the calendar is updated.

The platform also has celebrity/creator dashboards for managing calendars, services, prices, bookings, campaigns, earnings, and availability, plus customer dashboards for managing bookings and campaigns.

A key feature is creator verification. We want to verify both the person's identity and their ownership/control of the claimed social-media account, potentially using social account authorization, temporary verification codes, official website/social cross-links, and optional identity verification. Agencies should also be able to manage celebrity profiles as authorized representatives.

We also want AI features such as natural-language celebrity search, AI-powered creator matching based on budget/audience/availability, campaign brief generation, and AI assistance for planning influencer marketing campaigns.

The overall product should feel like a modern premium marketplace combining celebrity discovery, social-media advertising, event booking, real-time scheduling, payments, verification, and AI-powered matching.
